Our Story

Founded on the 25th of January, 2021

Limitless Schools was born from a deeply personal conviction that every child deserves more than ordinary. On the 25th of January 2021, we opened our doors with a small but passionate team, a handful of families who believed in us, and an unshakeable belief that limits only exist in the mind.

What began as a modest nursery and primary school has grown into a respected institution in Imo State, recognised for academic excellence, character development, and a genuine love for children.

Today, we serve hundreds of families across Owerri and continue to raise the bar for our pupils, our staff, and our community.
