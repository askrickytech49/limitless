Admissions

We warmly welcome applications for our Nursery and Primary programmes. Enrolment is open and spaces are limited.

The Process

How To Apply

01

Collect Application

Visit our school office in Owerri or request the application pack. Our admissions team will walk you through every detail.

02

Submit Documents

Return the completed form with all required documents and book an appointment for a guided visit.

03

Child Assessment

Your child attends a relaxed, age appropriate session designed to understand their strengths and personality.

04

Offer and Enrolment

Successful applicants receive a formal offer. Fee payment and official enrolment follow acceptance.

Documents Required

  • Completed admissions application form
  • Child's birth certificate, original and photocopy
  • Two recent passport sized photographs
  • Previous school report card for Primary applicants
  • Immunisation or vaccination card
  • Parent or Guardian valid government issued ID
